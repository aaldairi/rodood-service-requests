Store is the business itself: products, services, your orders and requests. The second phone is Rodood Business: the team replies, takes over from Noor, and creates quotations by giving Noor one line.

account_treeHow a client finds a business

  • Today: a business appears on Home only after it opens a request for the number, or after an order. The app has no link handler and nothing calls the follow API.
  • Proposed: universal links for app.rodoodai.com/s/<code> and /b/<code> open the app on this page; "Add a business" on Home scans the QR or pastes the link and calls POST /api/customer/stores/:code/follow.

auto_awesomeCreating a quotation

  • The team gives Noor one line ("Italy, 2 people, 10 days, 10% off") or a voice note. Noor picks the lines from the catalogue and services, adds official fees separately, applies the discount and a 7-day validity.
  • The preview is the exact card the client sees: title, lines, total, validity. The team can remove a line, change the discount or validity, then Send. Noor never sends a quotation on its own.
  • One document type: a quotation with an optional discount. A promotion on a product is a shared product at a discounted price, not a separate card. Accepted quotations lock and raise a draft invoice linked to them.

forumChat

  • One thread per business. Purple = Noor, orange = a person; the header dot shows who is replying now.
  • Message kinds: text, product, service, quotation (lines, validity, accept), invoice (optional quoteId, pay), image, file, audio, system.
  • Client composer: text, photo, camera, file, hold-to-record voice. Suggestion chips come from Noor.

storefrontStore

  • The store and services the business already has on Rodood, inside the page. Add to basket, checkout, start a request.
  • "Ask Noor" from Store jumps to Chat with the question prefilled; Noor replies with product cards.
  • Your requests and orders with this business sit at the bottom of Store, with live progress.
