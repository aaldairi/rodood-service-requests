Built-in rules for every business

  • Each business adds its own services from Services, starting blank or from the template library, and sets a fixed price or quotes each request.
  • Official fees (government, embassy, registry) always appear as their own line, separate from the business's charge. VAT applies to the charge only.
  • No screen promises approval or timing. Authority steps say the decision belongs to the authority.
  • Clients are only asked for what a step needs, and never for passwords or PINs.
  • Answers decide which steps apply, so each client only sees steps that fit their case.
  • Editing a service changes new requests only. Open requests keep their steps and price.
